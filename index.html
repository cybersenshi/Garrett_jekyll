Welcome To The Theatre!

Many people wonder whether or not they can learn to sing; they have the misconception that great singers are born great. However, musical talent is like any other talent in that one person might start out with different “innate” capabilities than the next person. Most people have some musical ability, and those that do can enhance this ability through education and practice. Developing vocal ability can be one of the most satisfying things you'll ever do! Many of my students work hard to develop their voices and become as talented as their idols. The same is true regarding acting. Hard work plus technique can open up a life upon the stage!

So come on! Life is an adventure - give voice and acting lessons a try. Who knows, you might be the next Pavarotti, or the next soloist in your Sunday choir!
