Singing

Garrett Liam States is one of America's most respected and experienced singing and acting coaches. His wealth and breadth of performances enable him to bring world class vocal techniques to the St. Louis area. These experiences, in addition to his extensive coaching and directing career, allow Garrett’s students to learn in an unparalleled professional setting.

Such experiences include:
Serving as the youngest baritone to solo with the St. Louis Symphony
A special invitation from the Italian Government to perform at the 2001 Puccini Festival
Concert work throughout Germany, Switzerland and Austria
Teaching experiences in Vienna and throughout Italy
An extensive Sound of Music tour with Marie Osmond in Hong Kong, Singapore and Bangkok
A special invitation to sing at the National Symphonic Ball at the Kennedy Center in Washington D.C.
Training at the MET Studios in NYC with John Reardon
Masters classes with opera greats Pavarotti, Sutherland, Horne, Milnes and others
Additional projects currently in progress
Garrett currently coaches vocal teachings of jazz, opera and musical theater, ages 10 and up.

His students range from aspiring young professionals to students ready for ongoing coaching for upcoming productions, auditions and performances. In addition to teaching master techniques, Garrett’s lessons include teaching students how to be directable and how to prepare themselves for future endeavors. His extensive experiences allow him to serve as one of the highest quality singing coach in St. Louis.

“When a singer truly feels and experiences what the music is all about, the words will automatically ring true.” -Montserrat Caballe

Opera

Concerts International Vocal & Acting Studio is one of the premier institutions with the professional experience and educational facilitation required for opera coaching excellence. The highly acclaimed Garrett Liam States provides an individualized experience to help opera students and professionals perform to the highest level of their potential.

Having performed in countries around the world, Garrett has the wealth and breadth of diverse experiences to help prepare you for any number of performance challenges you might face.

His wide range of performance experiences include:
Singing at the National Symphonic Ballat the Kennedy Center in Washington D.C., for an event honoring the Italian Ambassador (he was invited by the Italian Ambassador for this event)
Performing at the Puccini Festival in Torre del Lago, Italy, where he was invited by the Italian Cultural Department
Performing for the Royal Family of Thailand
Playing opposite Marie Osmond, as Captain Von Trapp in The Sound of Music in states across Asia

Such performances were shaped by Garrett's strong educational background, which includes studies at the MET Studios in NYC with John Reardon and taking Master Classes with opera greats Pavarotti, Sutherland, Horne, Milnes, among others.

With Garrett's help, you will be far more prepared than your peers for rewarding, professional careers in opera performance and education, no matter the venue.
